# User Manual

## Signing in
Use the e-mail and temporary password from HR, then change your password under **My profile → Security**. Forgot it? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page or ask HR to reset it. Use **Sign out of all devices** if you used a shared computer.

## Layout
- **Left sidebar** – modules available to your role.
- **Top bar** – search (tasks, people, projects), reporting **period selector** (applies to dashboards and reports), **Quick add task** (managers), **notification bell** (updates live), profile menu.

## Employees

### My Tasks
Shows everything assigned to you. Filter by status, priority, due window, project, department or category, and switch between **Table**, **Board** and **Calendar**.

Open a task to see details, attachments, comments and its full history. Actions:
- **Update progress** – set % complete, status (Not Started / In Progress / Blocked), hours spent and a note. *Blocked* requires a description; your supervisor is notified.
- **Submit for review** – when finished, add a summary and evidence. The task becomes *Under Review* and your supervisor is notified. Tasks with unfinished dependencies or subtasks cannot be submitted.
- **Attach file** – PDF, Office, images, CSV, ZIP up to 10 MB; tick *supporting evidence* for proof of completion.
- **Comments** – ask questions or post updates.

If changes are requested, the task returns to *In Progress* with the supervisor's feedback. Once approved it is *Completed* and locked; only a manager can reopen it (with a reason).

A task shows **Overdue** automatically when its due date passes and it is not completed or cancelled. You receive reminders before the deadline (by default 3 days and 1 day before, and on the day).

On the **Board**, drag cards between *Not Started*, *In Progress*, *Blocked* (asks for a reason) and *Under Review* (submits).

### Daily Planner
1. Add today's activities (optionally linked to a task) with the expected deliverable and estimated hours.
2. During/at the end of the day mark each as Completed / Pending / Blocked and enter actual hours.
3. Note obstacles; tick **I need assistance** and describe the help needed.
4. **Submit daily report**. Use **Carry over pending** the next day.
The summary shows planned, completed, pending, blocked, actual hours and completion %.

### Performance
**Performance** shows your scorecard: this month's automatic indicators, the latest finalized review score and category, KPI breakdown, 12-month trend and your manager's feedback and action plan. You cannot edit scores. Draft reviews are visible only after your manager finalizes them.

How scores work: completion and on-time delivery come from your tasks; cancelled tasks, approved extensions and delays caused by external factors or management are not held against you; if there are too few tasks in a period the result is shown as **Insufficient data**.

## Team Leads / Supervisors
Everything above, plus:
- **Team Tasks** – all tasks for your team members and tasks you assigned.
- **Create tasks** – *Quick add* (top bar) or **New task** (adds description, recurrence, dependencies). Select several people and choose *one shared task* or *a separate task for each person*. You can only assign to your own team.
- **Review submissions** – *Approve* (quality rating required) or *Request changes* (feedback required). On the Board, dragging to *Completed* opens the approval dialog.
- **More actions** (⋯ on a task): edit details, change deadline (reason + delay cause; the original date is kept), reassign, classify delay, escalate to HR management, cancel (reason).
- **Daily Planner → Team daily reports** – see each member's plan, completion, obstacles and help requests; who hasn't submitted.
- **Employees** – your people with live counters and profiles.
- **Workload** – utilisation per person; select someone to reassign their open tasks.
- **Performance** – team scorecards; **Start review** to evaluate a member for a month, quarter or year.
- **Reports** – all ten reports for your team, CSV export and print.
- **Projects** – create projects you own, add milestones and members, track progress and the timeline.

## Notifications
You are notified when a task is assigned, a deadline approaches or passes, a task is submitted, changes are requested, a task is approved, reassigned, its deadline changes, it becomes blocked, and for comments, escalations, delayed milestones and published reviews. Turn individual types off in **Notifications → Notification settings**.

## Reports (managers)
1. Daily Employee Activity · 2. Weekly Team Performance · 3. Monthly Employee Performance · 4. Overdue Tasks · 5. Task Completion · 6. Employee Workload · 7. Project Progress · 8. Department-wise Performance · 9. Individual Employee History · 10. Pending & Unresolved Tasks.
Choose filters, then **Export CSV (Excel)** or **Print** (print layout hides navigation and adds the company name and generation time).
